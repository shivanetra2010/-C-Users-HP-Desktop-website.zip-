HOW TO MAKE YOUR WEBSITE LIVE FOR FREE IN 60 SECONDS
========================================================================

OPTION 1: NETLIFY DROP (Easiest - No Login / Registration Required!)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your browser and go to: https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag and drop the "www" folder from your Desktop into the box on Netlify.
3. Done! Netlify will immediately give you a live HTTPS link (e.g. https://chandra-sekhar-resume.netlify.app) that you can share with recruiters!

OPTION 2: VERCEL (Fast & Professional)
---------------------------------------
1. Go to: https://vercel.com
2. Sign up or log in.
3. Click "Add New Project" -> Drag and drop your "www" folder.
4. Your site will be live instantly with a free custom URL.

OPTION 3: GITHUB PAGES
----------------------
1. Create a free account on https://github.com
2. Create a new public repository named "resume".
3. Upload all files from your "www" folder.
4. Go to Settings -> Pages -> Source: "main" branch.
5. Your live link will be: https://yourusername.github.io/resume

========================================================================
HOW TO CHANGE YOUR PROFILE PHOTO ON THE LIVE SITE:
========================================================================
- On your live website, simply hover over the profile picture and click "Upload Photo".
- Choose your photo from your computer. It will immediately display and save on your site!
